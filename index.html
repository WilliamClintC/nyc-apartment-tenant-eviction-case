  • One agent's affirmation says the tenant "consented to pay" arrears; the stipulation waived them if he left on time.
  • Lease history is inconsistent across the petition, ledger and tenant's papers. The petition does not mention the 2024 lease.
  • Notice of Petition says the case is "for a reason other than not paying your rent"; the notice cites arrears.

Only a marshal can carry out an eviction

General background Not from the file.

  • In New York a landlord cannot evict by self-help: no changing locks, no cutting off access or essential services (RPAPL 768).
  • After a court issues a warrant, a city marshal carries out the eviction, after serving notice.
  • A tenant who is locked out without a marshal can file a lockout case (the tenant here did, on Sep 10; it was dismissed without prejudice on Sep 14).
  • The access cut-offs the tenant alleges are claims in the file, not findings.
